## Proposed Page Types (ranked by opportunity)

### 1. Rêver de son ex : signification — HIGH ⭐
- **Pattern**: `/rupture/rever-de-son-ex/` (or `/sentiments/`)
- **Search intent**: informational + spiritual (strongly voyance-aligned)
- **Keyword pattern**: "rêver de son ex signification", "rêver de son ex qui revient", "rêver de son ex qui nous ignore", "signification rêve ex qui embrasse"
- **Why it's the best gap**: an **English** page already exists (`/en/dream-about-ex-meaning`) with **no French equivalent** — the demand is validated internally, and dream interpretation sits squarely in the voyance wheelhouse. Very high FR volume, low keyword difficulty.
- **Content uniqueness**: High — dream-symbol interpretation matrix (ex qui revient / qui ignore / qui embrasse / qui pleure / mort de l'ex), none of which any current page touches.
- **Feasibility**: Ready. No new data needed.

### 2. Mon ex refait sa vie / est déjà avec quelqu'un — MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Pattern**: `/rupture/ex-refait-sa-vie/`
- **Search intent**: informational / emotional coping
- **Keyword pattern**: "mon ex refait sa vie", "mon ex est déjà avec quelqu'un", "mon ex est passé à autre chose", "accepter que mon ex refasse sa vie"
- **Distinct from**: P3 (`quitte-pour-quelquun-dautre`) is about being **left FOR** someone; this is the ex **moving on AFTER** the breakup and how to cope / whether it means it's truly over. Different emotional job.
- **Content uniqueness**: Medium-High — coping framework + "does moving on prove it's over?" (answer: no certainty). Must cross-link P3 to avoid overlap.
- **Feasibility**: Ready.

### 3. Comment réagir quand son ex reprend contact / faut-il répondre — MEDIUM
- **Pattern**: `/reconquete/repondre-a-son-ex/`
- **Search intent**: actionable / decision
- **Keyword pattern**: "faut-il répondre à son ex", "mon ex me recontacte que faire", "comment répondre à son ex", "mon ex m'envoie un message"
- **Distinct from**: `ex-revient-silence-radio` (ex recontacts after *your* no-contact, reconquest framing) — this is the reader **receiving** contact and deciding **whether/how to respond**, independent of a reconquest strategy.
- **Content uniqueness**: Medium — a "should I reply / how / when to stay silent" decision guide. Moderate adjacency risk with silence-radio pages; needs careful framing + cross-links.
- **Feasibility**: Ready.

### 4. Revoir son ex après la rupture — MEDIUM (optional)
- **Pattern**: `/reconquete/revoir-son-ex/`
- **Keyword pattern**: "revoir son ex après une rupture", "comment se comporter en revoyant son ex", "revoir son ex fait mal"
- **Content uniqueness**: Medium — how to prepare for and behave at a first in-person meeting after a breakup. Some adjacency with ex-qui-revient; narrower (the meeting itself).
- **Feasibility**: Ready.
